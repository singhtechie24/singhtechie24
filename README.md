## 🚀 Featured projects

### ⏱️ Hustle — a live web app for UK workers with more than one job
Built after juggling two rotas, two payrolls and no idea whether I had Friday off.

- Every shift from every job in one calendar
- Take-home pay estimate for every payday (UK PAYE and National Insurance)
- Import a rota from a screenshot with AI, checked before anything is saved
- 3,600+ automated tests, with required CI checks on every change

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Firebase` `OpenAI` `Vitest` `Playwright`

**[Live app](https://www.hustlepay.co.uk)** · [Case study](https://balbir.dev/work/hustle?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme) · ![Public early access](https://img.shields.io/badge/public-early_access-2ea44f?style=flat-square)

### 🎙️ EchoFlow — a local AI voice-dictation app for Windows
Hold a key, speak, release, and your words appear wherever you're typing.

- Speech recognition runs on your PC, no cloud needed
- Optional local AI clean-up of what you said
- 11.2 MB installer after removing unused CUDA files
- Signed auto-updates, plus a product website with 308 Playwright tests

`Tauri` `Rust` `React` `TypeScript` `Next.js`

**[Website](https://echoflow.uk)** · [Case study](https://balbir.dev/work/echoflow?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme) · ![Public beta](https://img.shields.io/badge/public-beta-6d5ef5?style=flat-square)

### 🩺 HealthAssist — an AI health and wellness companion (MSc dissertation)
Contextual conversations, live voice and personal wellness tracking in one place.

- Contextual AI chat and live voice
- Mood, medication and wellness tracking
- AI rules, quotas and limits enforced on the server
- 146 tests across three suites

`React` `TypeScript` `Firebase` `Cloudflare Workers` `OpenAI`

**[Live demo](https://healthassist-mvp.vercel.app/)** · [Case study](https://balbir.dev/work/healthassist?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme) · ![MSc prototype](https://img.shields.io/badge/MSc-prototype-0A66C2?style=flat-square)

<sub>Educational and non-clinical: it doesn't diagnose or replace professional healthcare.</sub>

### 📅 Doctor Appointment — find a doctor, book a slot, manage it
My B.Tech final-year project, later revisited and hardened.

- Doctor search, availability and authenticated booking
- Bookings checked on the server, so you only see your own

`Next.js` `React` `Strapi` `PostgreSQL` `Kinde`

**[Live demo](https://doctor-appointment-booking-web-nextjs-two.vercel.app/)** · [Case study](https://balbir.dev/work/doctor-appointment?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme) · [Code](https://github.com/singhtechie24/Doctor-Appointment-Booking-Web-Nextjs)
